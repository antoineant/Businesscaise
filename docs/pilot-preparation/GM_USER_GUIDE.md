## Getting Started

### What is BusinessCaise?

BusinessCaise is an interactive business simulation platform where student teams run virtual companies over 10 sessions (typically spanning 5 days). As Game Master, you control the game flow, create scenarios, monitor performance, and provide feedback.

### System Requirements

- **Browser:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest version)
- **Internet:** Stable connection required
- **Screen:** 1920x1080 or higher recommended

### Accessing the Platform

1. Navigate to the BusinessCaise GM Dashboard URL provided by your administrator
2. Click **"Register"** if this is your first time
3. Fill in your details:
   - **Name:** Your full name
   - **Email:** Your institutional email
   - **Password:** Strong password (min. 8 characters)
   - **Role:** Select **"Game Master"**
4. Click **"Login"** for subsequent visits

**Pro Tip:** Bookmark the dashboard URL for quick access.

## Creating Your First Game

### Step 1: Navigate to Create Game

1. From the main dashboard, click **"Create New Game"**
2. You'll see the game creation wizard

### Step 2: Basic Information

Fill in the game details:

- **Game Name:** A descriptive name (e.g., "MBA 2025 - Fall Cohort")
- **Description:** Brief overview of the simulation (shown to teams)
- **Duration:** Select 10 sessions (standard) or customize
- **Start Date:** When the game begins
- **End Date:** Final date for submissions

**Example:**
```
Name: MBA Business Strategy Simulation
Description: A 5-day intensive simulation where teams manage a SaaS startup through growth and challenges
Duration: 10 sessions
Start Date: November 20, 2025
End Date: November 24, 2025
```

### Step 3: Choose Company Scenario

Select the **Company Archetype** (starting conditions):

1. **Early-Stage Startup** 🚀
   - Best for: Entrepreneurship courses
   - Characteristics: Low metrics (30-40), limited resources, high risk
   - Focus: Growth, funding, product-market fit

2. **Product Launch** 📦
   - Best for: Marketing/operations courses
   - Characteristics: Medium metrics (50-60), established base
   - Focus: Scaling, brand management, customer acquisition

3. **Turnaround/Revival** ⚠️
   - Best for: Crisis management courses
   - Characteristics: Low metrics (20-40), urgent issues
   - Focus: Stabilization, cost-cutting, rebuilding trust

4. **Scale-Up/Hyper-Growth** 📈
   - Best for: Operations/strategy courses
   - Characteristics: High metrics (60-75), rapid expansion
   - Focus: Scaling operations, maintaining quality, managing growth

5. **Innovation/R&D Focus** 🔬
   - Best for: Innovation/strategy courses
   - Characteristics: Balanced metrics (50-60), long-term focus
   - Focus: Innovation, competitive positioning, strategic pivots

Select the **Industry Type** (business context):

- **Tech SaaS:** Subscription software (MRR, churn, user growth)
- **E-Commerce/Retail:** Online sales (inventory, logistics, suppliers)
- **Food & Beverage:** Restaurants/food products (compliance, quality)
- **Healthcare/Wellness:** Health services (regulations, outcomes)
- **Professional Services:** Consulting/agencies (utilization, billable hours)
- **Education/EdTech:** Learning platforms (engagement, retention)
- **Manufacturing/Hardware:** Physical products (supply chain, quality)
- **Custom/Open-Ended:** Flexible (define your own)

**Optional Fields:**
- **Company Name:** Custom name for the simulated company
- **Product Description:** What the company sells/offers

**Example Selection:**
```
Archetype: Early-Stage Startup
Industry: Tech SaaS
Company Name: CloudFlow Inc.
Product: Project management software for remote teams
```

### Step 4: Configure Pod Competition (Optional)

Pods allow teams to compete in smaller groups (recommended for classes >12 teams):

- **Enable Pods:** Toggle ON
- **Pod Size:** 4 teams per pod (recommended)
- **Assignment Method:**
  - **Random:** Fair distribution (recommended for most cases)
  - **Manual:** You assign teams later
  - **Balanced:** Skill-based (future feature)

**When to use pods:**
- Classes with 16+ students (4+ teams)
- Want more focused competition
- Multiple small "winners" instead of one overall winner

**Example:**
```
Enable Pods: YES
Pod Size: 4 teams
Assignment Method: Random
Enable Category Awards: YES
```

### Step 5: Review & Create

1. Review all settings
2. Click **"Create Game"**
3. The system automatically generates 10 sessions
4. You're redirected to the game details page

**Pro Tip:** You can edit game settings later from the game details page.

## Managing Sessions

### Understanding Sessions

Each game has **10 sessions** representing decision points throughout the simulation:

| Session | Typical Day | Period | Common Theme |
|---------|-------------|--------|--------------|
| 1 | Monday | AM | Crisis Management |
| 2 | Monday | PM | Resource Allocation |
| 3 | Tuesday | AM | Market Expansion |
| 4 | Tuesday | PM | Investor Pitch |
| 5 | Wednesday | AM | Product Launch |
| 6 | Wednesday | PM | Compliance Issue |
| 7 | Thursday | AM | Competitive Response |
| 8 | Thursday | PM | Strategic Pivot |
| 9 | Friday | AM | Final Quarter |
| 10 | Friday | PM | Final Presentations |

### Unlocking Sessions

Sessions are locked by default. Unlock them when you're ready:

1. Navigate to **"Sessions"** tab in game details
2. Click **"Unlock"** next to the session
3. Teams can now view the challenge and submit

**Best Practice:** Unlock sessions at specific times (e.g., 9:00 AM each morning).

### Editing Session Content

Click **"Edit"** to customize:

- **Title:** Session name (e.g., "Session 1: Morning Crisis")
- **Description:** Context and scenario details
- **Deadline:** When submissions close
- **Challenge Text:** The decision prompt for teams

**Example Challenge:**
```
Title: Session 1 - Product Crisis
Description: A critical bug has been discovered in your main product. Customer complaints are flooding in. You need to decide how to respond.

Challenge:
Your engineering team estimates 2 days to fix the bug properly, but customers are threatening to cancel. Your options:

1. Push a quick patch (1 hour) - May not fully solve the issue
2. Full fix (2 days) - Customers will be unhappy with wait
3. Offer refunds/discounts while fixing - Costs money
4. Public apology + transparency - Builds trust but admits failure

What's your strategy? (Explain your reasoning in 200-300 words)
```

### Session States

- **Locked:** Teams cannot view (default state)
- **Unlocked:** Teams can view and submit
- **Closed:** Deadline passed (submissions still visible to you)

## Creating Narratives

Narratives are story elements that enhance immersion. There are **4 types:**

### 1. Briefings 📋
**Purpose:** Morning updates, session introductions
**When to use:** Start of each session
**Example:**
```
Title: Monday Morning Briefing
Content: Good morning team! Here's what's happening today:

• Customer tickets: 47 open (up 20% from Friday)
• Cash runway: 8 months remaining
• Marketing campaign goes live at 2 PM
• Board meeting scheduled for Thursday

Today's priority: Address the support backlog before it impacts satisfaction scores.
```

### 2. News 📰
**Purpose:** External events, market changes, competitor actions
**When to use:** Mid-session, to introduce new factors
**Example:**
```
Title: TechCrunch: Competitor Raises $50M Series B
Content: Your main competitor, FastTrack Solutions, just announced a $50M Series B funding round. According to their press release, they plan to:

• Double their engineering team
• Launch 3 new product features by Q2
• Aggressive customer acquisition campaign

Industry analysts predict intensified competition in your market segment.
```

### 3. Emails ✉️
**Purpose:** Communication from NPCs (board members, customers, employees)
**When to use:** To add character perspectives
**Example:**
```
Title: Email from Sarah Chen, Head of Customer Success
From: sarah.chen@yourcompany.com
Content: Hi team,

I wanted to give you a heads up - I just got off the phone with three of our largest clients. They're all asking about the new feature set that FastTrack announced.

Two of them mentioned they're "evaluating alternatives." We need to respond strategically here.

Can we discuss this afternoon?

Best,
Sarah
```

### 4. Alerts 🚨
**Purpose:** Urgent notifications, critical issues
**When to use:** Time-sensitive events, crises
**Example:**
```
Title: URGENT: Server Outage Detected
Content: ⚠️ CRITICAL ALERT

Our primary servers are experiencing an outage. Current status:

• Services down: 23 minutes
• Customers affected: ~2,400 (80% of user base)
• Support tickets: 156 and climbing
• Twitter mentions: #YourCompanyDown is trending

DevOps team is investigating. Estimated time to resolution: Unknown.

IMMEDIATE ACTION REQUIRED: How do you communicate with customers?
```

### Creating a Narrative

1. Go to **"Narratives"** tab in game details
2. Click **"Create New Narrative"**
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Type:** Select from 4 types above
   - **Title:** Headline (keep it concise)
   - **Content:** Main text (use formatting for readability)
   - **Author:** Optional (e.g., "CEO", "Board Chair")
   - **Target Teams:**
     - **All Teams:** Everyone receives it
     - **Specific Teams:** Select individual teams

4. Click **"Create"**

**Pro Tip:** Use targeted narratives to give struggling teams hints or challenge leading teams with harder scenarios.

### Read Statistics

Track which teams have read your narratives:
- **Read Count:** How many teams have opened it
- **Unread Count:** Teams who haven't seen it yet
- **Read By:** List of specific teams

## Using Reality Lens (AI Enhancement)

Reality Lens uses Perplexity AI to enhance your narratives with real-world data and context.

### Tab 1: Enhance Narrative

Make your narratives more realistic with current business context:

1. Navigate to **"Reality Lens"** tab
2. Select **"Enhance Narrative"** tab
3. Write or paste your draft narrative
4. Select **Industry** and **Archetype** (auto-filled from game settings)
5. Click **"Enhance with AI"**

**What it does:**
- Adds real-world business context
- Includes current market trends
- Provides data citations from reputable sources
- Suggests realistic numbers and scenarios

**Example Input:**
```
Your company needs to decide on a pricing strategy for your new product.
```

**AI-Enhanced Output:**
```
Your company needs to decide on a pricing strategy for your new SaaS product. According to recent SaaS pricing research (ProfitWell, 2024), most B2B SaaS companies use value-based pricing, with average prices ranging from $49-499/month depending on feature tiers.

Industry benchmark data shows:
• Freemium conversion rates: 2-5% (OpenView Partners)
• Annual vs. monthly pricing: 20% discount typical for annual
• Customer acquisition cost in SaaS: $1.18 per $1 of LTV (acceptable ratio)

Your competitors in the project management space show varied strategies:
• Asana: $10.99-24.99/user/month
• Monday.com: $8-16/user/month
• ClickUp: Freemium to $19/user/month

Consider your positioning, target customer willingness to pay, and cost structure.

Sources:
[1] ProfitWell SaaS Pricing Survey 2024
[2] OpenView SaaS Benchmarks Report
[3] Competitor pricing pages (accessed Nov 2025)
```

**Best Practice:** Review the AI output and adjust tone/complexity for your students.

### Tab 2: Event Inspiration

Get AI-generated event ideas to keep your simulation dynamic:

1. Select **"Event Inspiration"** tab
2. Choose **Industry** and **Event Type:**
   - **Crisis:** Urgent problems requiring immediate response
   - **Opportunity:** Positive events with potential upside
   - **Decision:** Strategic choices with trade-offs
   - **External:** Market changes, competitor actions
   - **Internal:** Team/culture/operations challenges

3. Click **"Get Event Ideas"**

**What you get:**
- 5 event scenarios with descriptions
- Suggested metric impacts
- Real-world context

**Example Ideas (Tech SaaS, Crisis):**
```
1. Data Breach Discovery
   Description: Security team discovered unauthorized access to customer database 48 hours ago. Estimated 2,500 user records potentially compromised. You must decide: immediate public disclosure vs. internal investigation first.
   Suggested Impacts: Customer Satisfaction -15, Operations -10

2. Key Engineer Resignation
   Description: Your lead engineer (who built 60% of core infrastructure) resigned effective immediately to join a competitor. Knowledge transfer is minimal.
   Suggested Impacts: Operations -20, HR -10

3. Critical API Outage
   Description: Your main API service has been down for 3 hours during peak usage. 80% of customers affected. Twitter mentions spiking.
   Suggested Impacts: Customer Satisfaction -25, Operations -15

[... 2 more ideas ...]
```

**Using the Ideas:**
- Click **"Select"** to load the idea
- Edit as needed for your class context
- Add to your narrative schedule

### Rate Limits

- **Reality Lens:** Unlimited for GMs
- **Ask the Market (Teams):** 5 queries per team per session

Monitor usage from the **"Perplexity Usage"** section in game details.

## Reviewing & Scoring Submissions

### Viewing Submissions

1. Go to **"Submissions"** tab in game details
2. Filter by status:
   - **All:** Everything
   - **Pending:** Need your review
   - **Scored:** Already graded

### Scoring Process

Click any submission to open the details page:

1. **Review Submission:**
   - Team name and session
   - Submission timestamp
   - Full text response (or file download)
   - Current team metrics

2. **Provide Score (0-100):**
   - **90-100:** Exceptional strategic thinking
   - **80-89:** Strong, well-reasoned response
   - **70-79:** Good understanding, minor gaps
   - **60-69:** Adequate but lacking depth
   - **Below 60:** Weak reasoning or major misunderstandings

3. **Write Feedback:**
   - What they did well
   - Areas for improvement
   - Specific suggestions for next session

4. **Click "Submit Score"**

**What happens next:**
- Team metrics update automatically based on score
- Team receives notification via WebSocket
- Leaderboard updates in real-time

### Sample Scoring Rubric

| Criteria | Weight | What to Look For |
|----------|--------|------------------|
| **Strategic Thinking** | 40% | Long-term vision, trade-off analysis |
| **Justification** | 30% | Clear reasoning, data/evidence cited |
| **Feasibility** | 20% | Realistic given constraints |
| **Communication** | 10% | Clarity, structure, professionalism |

### Updating Scores

You can re-score submissions:
1. Return to the submission details page
2. Enter new score and update feedback
3. Click **"Update Score"**
4. Metrics recalculate automatically

**Pro Tip:** Use feedback to guide teams toward better strategic thinking in subsequent sessions.

## Pod Competition & Category Awards

### Pod Competition

If you enabled pods, teams compete primarily within their pod:

**Viewing Pod Leaderboards:**
1. Go to **"Teams"** tab
2. Filter by pod (e.g., "Pod Alpha")
3. See pod-specific rankings

**Managing Pods:**
- **Random Assignment:** Automatic when teams join
- **Manual Assignment:** Click "Manage Pods" to reassign teams
- **Pod Size:** Can adjust before teams join

**Benefits:**
- More competitive for each team (3 rivals vs. 7+)
- Multiple "winners" (1 per pod)
- Easier to track performance in large classes

### Category Awards

Six ways for teams to "win":

1. 💰 **Financial Excellence** - Highest financial metric
2. ⚙️ **Operations Leader** - Best operations efficiency
3. 📣 **Marketing Champion** - Strongest marketing performance
4. 👥 **Best Employer** - Top HR/employee satisfaction
5. 😊 **Customer Favorite** - Best customer satisfaction
6. 🏆 **Overall Champion** - Highest overall score

**Viewing Category Leaders:**
1. Go to **"Leaderboard"** tab
2. Select **"Category Rankings"** view
3. See top 3 teams in each category

**Scope Options:**
- **Pod Level:** Top teams within each pod
- **Global Level:** Top teams across entire game

**Best Practice:** Announce category awards during your final session to celebrate different types of excellence.

## Best Practices

### Before the Simulation

**1 Week Before:**
- ✅ Create and test the game
- ✅ Unlock Session 1
- ✅ Create at least 3 narratives for Day 1
- ✅ Prepare your scoring rubric
- ✅ Send teams the join link and player guide

**2 Days Before:**
- ✅ Test the join process yourself
- ✅ Ensure all session deadlines are correct
- ✅ Review Reality Lens features
- ✅ Prepare opening remarks

**Day Before:**
- ✅ Final check: Can teams access the join page?
- ✅ Send reminder email with start time
- ✅ Have troubleshooting guide ready

### During the Simulation

**Daily Routine:**
1. **Morning (15 min):**
   - Unlock that day's morning session
   - Create/send morning briefing narrative
   - Check for overnight submissions

2. **Mid-Morning (15 min):**
   - Review and score submissions
   - Monitor team progress
   - Send mid-morning news/emails if needed

3. **Lunch (10 min):**
   - Check leaderboard
   - Identify struggling teams
   - Send targeted support narratives

4. **Afternoon (variable):**
   - Unlock afternoon session
   - Continue scoring
   - Respond to team questions
   - Create afternoon events/challenges

5. **End of Day (15 min):**
   - Final scoring
   - Update leaderboard
   - Prepare next day's narratives

### Creating Effective Challenges

**Good Challenge Characteristics:**
- **Clear:** Teams understand what's being asked
- **Realistic:** Situations they might face in real business
- **Consequential:** Choice matters (no "right" answer)
- **Time-bound:** Deadline creates urgency
- **Aligned:** Connects to course learning objectives

**Example Structure:**
```
Context: (What's happening)
Situation: (The specific problem)
Constraints: (Limitations they face)
Options: (Possible approaches - optional)
Decision Required: (What they must submit)
```

### Engaging Narratives

**Do:**
- Use specific numbers and details
- Reference earlier team decisions
- Build narrative continuity
- Create characters students can relate to
- Introduce unexpected twists

**Don't:**
- Be too abstract or vague
- Overload with information
- Make scenarios too easy
- Forget about trailing teams
- Ignore your game's industry context

### Managing Pace

**If teams are struggling:**
- Provide more context in narratives
- Offer "hints" via targeted emails
- Reduce complexity of decisions
- Extend deadlines slightly

**If teams are breezing through:**
- Introduce more challenging scenarios
- Add time pressure
- Create competitive events
- Use Reality Lens for complex realistic situations

### Scoring Consistency

**Tips:**
- Score all teams for one session before moving to next
- Use your rubric consistently
- Take breaks between scoring sessions
- Save exemplar submissions to calibrate
- If uncertain, be generous (learning focus)

## FAQ

### Game Setup

**Q: Can I change the game settings after creating it?**
A: Yes, most settings can be edited from the game details page. Company scenario and pod settings should not be changed once teams have joined.

**Q: How many teams can join one game?**
A: No hard limit. Tested successfully with 40 teams (160 students). Use pods for classes >12 teams.

**Q: Can I run multiple games simultaneously?**
A: Yes, you can create and manage multiple games from your dashboard.

### Sessions

**Q: Can I unlock multiple sessions at once?**
A: Yes, but not recommended. Teams may rush ahead and miss the intended pacing.

**Q: What happens if a team misses a submission deadline?**
A: They can still submit (for partial credit if you choose), but the session remains accessible. You decide how to handle late submissions.

**Q: Can I delete or hide a session?**
A: You can edit but not delete. To skip a session, leave it locked and inform teams.

### Narratives

**Q: Do teams get notified when I create a narrative?**
A: Yes, if they're online they receive a real-time notification. Otherwise they see it when they log in.

**Q: Can I schedule narratives in advance?**
A: Not currently. Create and send in real-time for maximum impact.

**Q: What if I make a typo in a narrative?**
A: You can edit or delete narratives after creation.

### Scoring

**Q: How are team metrics calculated from scores?**
A: The system uses scoring algorithms that weight different factors. Generally:
- 90-100: Significant metric improvements
- 70-89: Moderate improvements
- 60-69: Slight improvements
- Below 60: Metric declines

**Q: Can students see their raw scores?**
A: No, students see their metrics (0-100 scale) and overall score, but not individual submission scores. This focuses them on holistic performance rather than "point hunting."

**Q: What if I accidentally give the wrong score?**
A: Simply re-score the submission. The system will recalculate metrics based on the new score.

### Technical

**Q: What if the platform goes down during the simulation?**
A: Submissions are saved immediately. If there's an issue, extend the deadline and inform students.

**Q: Can students access the simulation from mobile devices?**
A: Yes, the interface is responsive. Desktop is recommended for best experience.

**Q: Is there a "pause" feature if we need to take a break?**
A: Yes, use the "Pause Game" button on the game details page. Teams can still view content but cannot submit.

### Pedagogical

**Q: How much time should teams spend per session?**
A: 30-60 minutes typically. Morning sessions can be lighter (briefing + quick decision), afternoon sessions more complex.

**Q: Should I reveal the scoring rubric to students?**
A: Up to you. Transparency can help, but mystery can increase engagement. Consider sharing general criteria without exact weights.

**Q: How do I handle team conflicts or disputes?**
A: The platform doesn't track individual contributions. Manage team dynamics according to your normal classroom policies.
