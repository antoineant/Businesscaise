## Welcome to BusinessCaise!

BusinessCaise is a business simulation where your team runs a virtual company over 10 sessions. You'll face realistic business challenges, make strategic decisions, and compete against other teams.

### What You'll Do

- 📊 **Manage your company** with 5 key metrics (Financial, Operations, Marketing, HR, Customer Satisfaction)
- 📋 **Respond to challenges** in 10 decision-making sessions
- 📰 **Read business narratives** (briefings, news, emails, alerts)
- 💡 **Use AI research** to inform your decisions (Ask the Market)
- 🏆 **Compete** on leaderboards and category awards
- 📈 **Track progress** through metrics over time

### Game Structure

**10 Sessions** typically spread over **5 days:**

| Day | Morning Session | Afternoon Session |
|-----|-----------------|-------------------|
| Monday | Session 1 | Session 2 |
| Tuesday | Session 3 | Session 4 |
| Wednesday | Session 5 | Session 6 |
| Thursday | Session 7 | Session 8 |
| Friday | Session 9 | Session 10 (Final) |

**Each session:** 30-60 minutes of team work

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Receive Your Join Link

Your instructor will provide a unique game join link. It looks like:
```
https://businesscaise.com/join?gameCode=ABC123
```

**Important:** Everyone on your team uses the **same join link**.

### Step 2: Create Your Account

1. Click the join link
2. You'll see the game information
3. Click **"Register & Join"**
4. Fill in your details:
   - **Name:** Your full name
   - **Email:** Your student email
   - **Password:** Create a secure password
   - **Role:** Select **"Player"**
   - **Team Name:**
     - If you're the **first** person: Create a team name
     - If your teammates already joined: Select their team from dropdown

5. Click **"Join Game"**

**Pro Tip:** Coordinate with your team on who creates the team name first. Everyone else should join that existing team.

### Step 3: Access Your Dashboard

After joining:
- Bookmark the dashboard URL
- You'll automatically be on your team's dashboard
- Your team ID and game info are displayed

## Reading Narratives

Narratives provide context and story for your decisions. Check regularly!

### How to Access

1. Click the **"Narratives"** tab
2. You'll see an inbox-style interface

### Narrative Types

**📋 Briefings** - Morning updates, session context
- Read these **first** each session
- Set the stage for the day

**📰 News** - Market events, competitor actions
- External factors affecting your business
- May require strategic response

**✉️ Emails** - Communications from characters
- CEO, Board Members, Department Heads, Customers
- Provides different perspectives

**🚨 Alerts** - Urgent notifications
- Time-sensitive issues
- Often require immediate decisions

### Reading Interface

**Left Panel:** List of narratives
- **Blue dot** = unread
- **Bold text** = unread
- Filter by type (All, Briefings, News, Emails, Alerts)

**Right Panel:** Full narrative content
- Click any narrative to read
- Auto-marks as read when you open it

**Pro Tip:** Click "Mark All Read" after reviewing to clear your inbox.

### Stay Updated

- Check narratives before making decisions
- New narratives appear in real-time
- Unread count badge shows on tab

## Making Decisions & Submissions

### Finding Active Challenges

1. Click **"Current Challenge"** tab
2. If a session is unlocked, you'll see:
   - Session title and description
   - Challenge prompt
   - Deadline
   - Submission form

### Reading the Challenge

**Typical structure:**
- **Context:** What's happening in your company
- **Situation:** The specific problem or opportunity
- **Decision Required:** What you need to submit

**Example:**
```
SESSION 3: Market Expansion Opportunity

Context: Your product is gaining traction in your home market. Several customers have requested expansion to the European market.

Situation: Expanding internationally requires significant investment:
• $150,000 for localization and compliance
• 3 months of engineering time
• New customer support infrastructure
• Currency/payment processing setup

However, early research shows potential for 30% revenue growth within 12 months.

Decision Required: Should you expand to Europe now, wait, or pass? Explain your strategy and reasoning (250-400 words).
```

### Making Your Decision

**Best Practices:**

1. **Read all narratives first** - They provide critical context

2. **Discuss as a team:**
   - What are the pros/cons?
   - What do we know vs. don't know?
   - What are the risks?
   - How does this fit our strategy?

3. **Use Ask the Market:**
   - Research market data
   - Get competitive insights
   - Validate assumptions

4. **Draft your response:**
   - State your decision clearly
   - Explain your reasoning
   - Consider trade-offs
   - Show strategic thinking

5. **Review before submitting:**
   - Check word count
   - Proofread
   - Ensure you answered the question

### Submitting

1. Type or paste your response in the text field
2. (If file upload required) Attach your file
3. Click **"Submit Decision"**
4. You'll see a confirmation message

**Important:**
- ✅ You can submit before the deadline
- ✅ Your GM will review and provide feedback
- ✅ Your metrics will update based on your performance
- ⚠️ Late submissions may receive reduced credit (check with your instructor)

### Submission Tips

**Strong submissions:**
- Answer the question directly
- Use data/evidence when possible
- Consider multiple perspectives
- Show awareness of constraints
- Communicate clearly

**Weak submissions:**
- Vague or generic answers
- No justification for choices
- Ignoring key information from narratives
- Poor writing/organization

## Using Ask the Market (AI Assistant)

Ask the Market is your AI-powered research tool. Use it to gather real-world business insights!

### How to Use

1. Click **"Ask the Market"** tab
2. Type your question in the text box
3. Click **"Ask"** (or press Enter)
4. Wait for the AI response (15-30 seconds)

### What You Can Ask

**Good Questions:**
- "What are typical customer acquisition costs in SaaS?"
- "How do successful companies handle data breaches?"
- "What pricing strategies work for B2B software?"
- "What are key metrics for e-commerce businesses?"

**Less Useful Questions:**
- "What should we do?" (Too vague)
- "What's the right answer?" (No single answer)
- Simple facts you can Google

### Understanding Responses

**You'll receive:**
- **Answer:** AI-powered response with real-world context
- **Confidence:** High/Medium/Low (how reliable the answer is)
- **Sources:** Links to original data (click to verify)
- **Related Questions:** Follow-up questions you might want to ask

**Pro Tip:** Always read the sources to understand context. Don't just copy the AI answer - use it to inform your thinking.

### Rate Limits

- **5 queries per team per session**
- Queries remaining shown at top
- Resets each new session
- Choose questions wisely!

**Strategy:**
- Use queries for important decisions
- Team should agree on questions before asking
- Save 1-2 queries for unexpected needs

### Query History

- View past questions and answers
- Click any previous question to review
- Useful for referencing earlier research

## Understanding Metrics

Your company performance is measured across **5 metrics** (0-100 scale):

### 1. 💰 Financial

**What it measures:** Revenue, profitability, cash flow, financial health

**Goes up when:**
- Strong revenue decisions
- Cost management
- Profitable strategies
- Wise investments

**Goes down when:**
- Excessive spending
- Poor pricing decisions
- Financial mismanagement

### 2. ⚙️ Operations

**What it measures:** Efficiency, processes, quality, execution capability

**Goes up when:**
- Process improvements
- Quality control
- Efficient resource use
- Technical excellence

**Goes down when:**
- System failures
- Quality issues
- Inefficient processes
- Operational mistakes

### 3. 📣 Marketing

**What it measures:** Brand strength, market position, customer acquisition

**Goes up when:**
- Effective campaigns
- Strong positioning
- Market share growth
- Brand building

**Goes down when:**
- Poor messaging
- Losing market share
- Weak competitive response
- Brand damage

### 4. 👥 HR (Human Resources)

**What it measures:** Employee satisfaction, culture, talent retention

**Goes up when:**
- Good leadership
- Fair policies
- Employee development
- Positive culture

**Goes down when:**
- High turnover
- Poor morale
- Unfair treatment
- Toxic culture

### 5. 😊 Customer Satisfaction

**What it measures:** Customer happiness, loyalty, retention, NPS

**Goes up when:**
- Great service
- Quality products
- Responsive support
- Customer-first decisions

**Goes down when:**
- Poor service
- Product issues
- Ignoring feedback
- Breaking trust

### Overall Score

Your **Overall Score** (0-100) is calculated from all 5 metrics:
- Weighted average
- Considers recent performance
- Updates after each scored submission

**Score Ranges:**
- **80-100:** Excellent (top performers)
- **65-79:** Good (solid performance)
- **50-64:** Average (room for improvement)
- **35-49:** Struggling (needs attention)
- **Below 35:** Crisis (urgent action needed)

### Metrics History

View your performance over time:
- Graph shows all 5 metrics
- Track trends
- See impact of decisions
- Identify strengths/weaknesses

**Pro Tip:** Don't obsess over small fluctuations. Focus on trends and strategic improvements.

See how all teams rank:

### Pod Competition (If Enabled)

Compete primarily within your pod (typically 4 teams):

### Category Awards 🏆

**6 ways to win:**

1. **💰 Financial Excellence** - Top financial metric
2. **⚙️ Operations Leader** - Top operations metric
3. **📣 Marketing Champion** - Top marketing metric
4. **👥 Best Employer** - Top HR metric
5. **😊 Customer Favorite** - Top customer satisfaction metric
6. **🏆 Overall Champion** - Top overall score

**See rankings:**
- Click "Category Rankings" in Leaderboard tab

**Strategy:**
- You don't have to be #1 overall to win
- Focus on your strengths
- Multiple teams can earn recognition

## Tips for Success

### Before Each Session

✅ Read all new narratives
✅ Check leaderboard to see where you stand
✅ Review metrics trends
✅ Discuss strategy with team

### During Decision-Making

✅ Consider multiple perspectives
✅ Use Ask the Market for research
✅ Think long-term, not just short-term
✅ Don't ignore any metric
✅ Take notes on your strategy

### After Submission

✅ Review feedback when you receive it
✅ Discuss what worked/didn't work
✅ Adjust strategy for next session
✅ Don't let one bad score derail you

### Team Collaboration

**Best practices:**
- Assign roles (e.g., one person submits, all discuss)
- Schedule regular check-ins
- Document your strategy
- Listen to all team members
- Be respectful of different perspectives

**Avoid:**
- One person making all decisions
- Skipping discussion
- Ignoring quieter members
- Rushing decisions
- Blaming individuals for bad outcomes

### Strategic Thinking

**Think like a real executive:**
- What are our constraints?
- What information do we need?
- What are the trade-offs?
- How does this align with our strategy?
- What could go wrong?

**Balance:**
- Short-term vs. long-term
- Risk vs. reward
- Different stakeholder needs
- All five metrics (don't neglect any)

## FAQ

### Getting Started

**Q: I lost my login link. How do I access the game?**
A: Bookmark the dashboard URL after your first login. If lost, ask your instructor for the join link again.

**Q: Can I join late if I missed the first session?**
A: Check with your instructor. You can technically join anytime, but you'll start with default metrics.

**Q: My teammate already created our team. How do I join?**
A: During registration, select your team from the dropdown instead of creating a new one.

### Submissions

**Q: Can I edit my submission after submitting?**
A: No, submissions are final. Review carefully before clicking submit.

**Q: What happens if we submit late?**
A: The platform accepts late submissions, but check your instructor's late policy.

**Q: Who should click "Submit" on our team?**
A: Anyone on your team can submit. Coordinate internally to avoid duplicate submissions.

**Q: Can we see our submission score?**
A: No, you see metric changes and feedback, but not raw scores. This focuses you on holistic performance.

### Narratives

**Q: Do we need to respond to narratives?**
A: Narratives provide context - use them to inform your challenge submissions. You don't submit separate responses to narratives.

**Q: Some narratives are targeted to specific teams. Will we know?**
A: You only see narratives sent to your team (or all teams). You won't know if others got different narratives.

### Ask the Market

**Q: Are the AI answers always correct?**
A: The AI uses reputable sources, but always verify. Check the confidence level and sources provided.

**Q: Can we use AI answers in our submissions?**
A: Yes, but cite them properly. Use AI for research, not to write your submission.

**Q: What if we run out of queries?**
A: You get 5 per session. Plan usage carefully. Queries reset each new session.

### Metrics & Competition

**Q: How are metrics calculated?**
A: Your instructor scores your submissions (0-100), and the system translates that into metric changes using algorithms that weight different factors.

**Q: Can metrics go negative?**
A: No, metrics floor at 0 and cap at 100.

**Q: What if we're in last place?**
A: Don't give up! Focus on improving your metrics, aim for category awards, and use it as a learning experience.

**Q: Are there prizes for winning?**
A: Check with your instructor. BusinessCaise is primarily an educational tool.

### Technical

**Q: Can we access from mobile phones?**
A: Yes, it's responsive. Desktop is recommended for submissions.

**Q: What if the site is down during our deadline?**
A: Contact your instructor immediately. They can extend deadlines.

**Q: Can multiple team members be logged in at once?**
A: Yes, but coordinate to avoid confusion. Only one person should submit.

### Pedagogy

**Q: Is there a "right answer" to challenges?**
A: No. You're evaluated on your reasoning and strategic thinking, not getting a specific answer.

**Q: How important is writing quality?**
A: Clear communication matters. Proofread and structure your responses well.

**Q: Can we use external resources besides Ask the Market?**
A: Check your instructor's policy. Generally, research is encouraged.
